ISOQUALITY ASSURED PROCESSPAN-INDIAMANUFACTURING & SITE REACH

KMS Infrastructures is a full-service prefab engineering company specializing in commercial, institutional and residential modular buildings — built off-site under controlled conditions, then transported and installed on your site in a fraction of the time conventional construction takes.

Every structure moves through a defined manufacturing process: design, fabrication, quality inspection, and site installation — overseen by a dedicated QA division working to international standards, so what leaves our facility matches exactly what was engineered.

From workmen accommodation to portable hospitals, factory sheds to smart modular homes — we've supported clients across energy, infrastructure, healthcare and education with structures engineered for their exact site and budget.

WHY PREFAB

Built Smarter, Not Just Faster

Off-site construction isn't a shortcut — it's a different discipline. Here's what it gets you.

01 / COST

Construction Savings

Controlled factory production cuts material waste and labour overhead, lowering total build cost versus conventional masonry.

02 / MOBILITY

Relocatable Assets

Structures are engineered to be disassembled and moved — your building becomes an asset, not a fixed liability.

03 / SPEED

Fast-Track Delivery

Fabrication happens in parallel with site prep. Most structures are installed and handed over in weeks, not months.

04 / EFFICIENCY

Lower Energy Build

Factory-controlled assembly reduces on-site energy consumption during construction by up to 67%.

05 / MATERIALS

Reusable Systems

Panels and frames are built for reuse and redeployment — ideal for phased projects and scaling operations.

06 / IMPACT

Reduced Site Waste

Off-site manufacturing significantly cuts the waste generated compared to traditional on-site construction.
